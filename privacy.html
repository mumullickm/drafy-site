Drafy · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Drafy collects nothing. There is nothing to collect.

Drafy is a free, offline QR-code generator. It is built so that none of your information ever leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Drafy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. We have no servers and no analytics. We never see anything you create.

Internet

Drafy does not use the internet. It has no network permission and works fully in airplane mode. Nothing you type or generate is uploaded anywhere.

Accounts

Drafy has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not know who you are.

Photos & files

When you choose to save a QR code, Drafy writes the image to your device's photo library. If you add a centre logo, Drafy reads the image you pick. These actions happen entirely on your device, only when you initiate them, and the images are never sent anywhere.

Advertising & tracking

Drafy contains no ads, no trackers, no third-party SDKs of that kind, and no advertising identifiers.

Children

Drafy is a general-purpose utility and is safe for all audiences. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
